## Methods checklist (standards Section 3)

- Base rates and exposure: the exposure base for every ash share is the inventoried tree population itself, printed with counts. The brief states prominently that the inventory covers Parks-managed trees in parks, medians, parkways, and along streets, and is not a census of all Denver trees; no claim is made about the city's separate 1-in-6 citywide figure beyond noting consistency on public land.
- Direction of causality: no causal claim is made. The What this might mean section carries the strongest alternative (planting history and inventory coverage) beside the exposure reading.
- Category and definition changes: the inventory is a standing snapshot with per-record inventory dates, not a time series; no trend is claimed, so no within-window category change applies. Condition is explicitly treated as recorded-at-inventory-date, not current.
- Exact date range: source pulled October 23, 2026. Recorded inventory dates run to June 30, 2026 at the latest, with 2 impossible pre-2000 values (both vacant-site records) documented in Data notes; no claim rests on the date field's extremes. The Denver 2027 budget dates in Who can act are confirmed in effect (Council release, denvergov.org, October 9, 2026).
- Joins: none. Neighborhood-name handling: the source carries 81 NEIGHBOR values including 3 pseudo values (Unassigned, N/A, blank) holding 4,464 tree records (1.5 percent); they are counted citywide, excluded from rankings, and printed in the brief.
- Small numbers: neighborhood rankings require at least 100 inventoried trees; every rate is printed with its counts. The smallest ranked denominator is CBD (1,401 trees). Park location counts are counts, not rates.
- Official definitions: "ash" uses the botanical genus Fraxinus, the genus EAB attacks, verified against the dataset's own botanical field; site designations and program eligibility language are the city's own as filed / as published on Be A Smart Ash, with truncations flagged where the source truncates.
- Layering admission test: no external layer was joined. The city context layer (EAB confirmation, June 2025; public-land 1-in-10 statement; treatment program descriptions) is admitted for two claims the inventory alone cannot make: that EAB is present in Denver (not merely a regional risk), and the control check in Finding 2. No other layer was needed; the STR / rental / parcel layers were considered and cut.
