## Package LinkedIn draft (for a future Friday synthesis, not today)

33,479 ash trees.

That is the ash count in Denver's own park and street tree inventory, counted tree by tree: 11.0% of the 305,295 trees the inventory records. Emerald ash borer, confirmed in Denver in June 2025, kills the genus Fraxinus. Nothing else in the dataset is at risk from it, and 1,154 records with "ash" in the name are not Fraxinus and are not counted here.

The exposure is not evenly spread. In Marston, 29.6% of inventoried trees are ash (815 of 2,757). In Indian Creek, 27.6% (924 of 3,349). The largest counts sit elsewhere: Gateway - Green Valley Ranch holds 2,374 ash trees, Central Park 1,961.

Two more findings from the same pull. 67.4% of the inventoried ash (22,574 trees) are street trees the inventory designates as privately maintained. And the dataset's own EAB field is blank for 87.1% of ash and otherwise holds 178 undefined values dataset-wide, so treatment coverage cannot be counted from the public data.
